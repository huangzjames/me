Pixoo Project Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Pixoo Project is a personal, non-commercial tool developed for my own Pinterest account. It helps me read Pins saved to a board, download newly saved images to my personal computer, and organize those images for manual editing in a local pixel-display GIF workspace.

1. Information the application uses

  • Pinterest authorization information required to access the account and board that I explicitly authorize.
  • Board and Pin information returned by Pinterest, such as Pin IDs, titles, timestamps, source links, and available image URLs.
  • Image files downloaded from the authorized board and local records used to identify previously synchronized Pins and avoid duplicate downloads.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to synchronize newly saved Pins to my personal computer, preserve their source information, and support local organization. The application does not publish Pins, create Pins, modify boards, run ads, or act on behalf of other users.

3. Storage and sharing

Authorization information and synchronization records are intended to be stored locally on my personal computer. The application does not sell, rent, or share Pinterest data with third parties, and does not use Pinterest data for advertising or profiling. The downloaded images remain local unless I choose to move or share them myself.

4. Retention and deletion

Local synchronization records and downloaded images can be deleted from the computer at any time. Pinterest access can also be revoked from the Pinterest account settings. Revoking access prevents future synchronization; it does not automatically remove files that were already saved locally, so those files must be deleted locally if desired.

5. Pinterest relationship

Pixoo Project is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Pinterest. Pinterest data is accessed only through authorization granted by the account owner and remains subject to Pinterest's own terms and policies.
